Combine advance billable lines into one invoice when billable lines are being composed together.
This process extension is available from 2025.01 (KB3552629).
Technical information for this process extension:
Usage:        Currently LN standard doesn't allow to compose advance billable lines
into one invoice and creates separate invoice for each advances.
With this Process Extension, it is possible to combine advance
billable lines when the billable lines are being composed into one
invoice.
